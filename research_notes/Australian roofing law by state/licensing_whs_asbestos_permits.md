## Q1. Which licence(s) are needed to do roofing work, per state, and do licence numbers have to be shown on quotes, contracts and advertising?

### Takeaway
Every jurisdiction splits roofing into two parts. (a) Roof cladding and tiling is building or trade work under a builder/trade licence. (b) Roof drainage (gutters, downpipes, flashings to stormwater) is plumbing in VIC, SA and TAS, while QLD puts rainwater goods under the "roof and wall cladding" building licence. The NT has no roof-plumbing licence at all. NSW and QLD both require the licence number in all advertising. NSW also has value thresholds: over $5,000 residential, and plumbing-type "specialist work" at any value.

### Cited Findings

**NSW (regulator: NSW Fair Trading / Building Commission NSW, Home Building Act 1989)**
- Residential building work over $5,000 (labour + materials incl. GST) requires a contractor licence. Specialist trades (electrical wiring, plumbing, draining, gas fitting, air-con/refrigeration) need a licence regardless of value. — [NSW Gov – Building trade advertisements](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/running-a-business/advertising-laws-and-your-business/building-trade-advertisements)
- Roof tiling and roof slating are separate licensed trade categories, each needing specified qualifications (e.g. Certificate III in Roof Tiling for tiling). — [NSW Gov – Roof tiling work](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/licences-and-credentials/building-and-trade-licences-and-registrations/roof-tiling-work); [NSW Gov – Roof slating work](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/licences-and-credentials/building-and-trade-licences-and-registrations/roof-slating-work)
- "Roof plumbing" is a defined licence category under Schedule 1 of the Home Building Act 1989 (definition text not retrieved). — [ABLIS – NSW Contractor Licence – Plumbing](https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/nsw/contractor-licence-plumbing-individual/16528)
- Advertising must show the licensee's name (or registered business name), licence number with the correct category of work, and business phone number. It is an offence to advertise residential work over $5,000, or specialist work of any value, without a current contractor licence. — [NSW Gov – Building trade advertisements](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/running-a-business/advertising-laws-and-your-business/building-trade-advertisements)
- These advertising details apply in every medium (print, radio, TV, internet, social media, vehicles). — [HIA – NSW site signage and advertising requirements](https://hia.com.au/resources-and-advice/managing-your-business/managing-compliance/articles/nsw-site-signage-and-advertising-requirements)

**VIC (regulator: Building and Plumbing Commission, BPC, which replaced the VBA)**
- "Roofing (stormwater)" is a plumbing class under Part 4 of the Plumbing Regulations 2018. It covers construction, installation, replacement, repair, alteration, maintenance, testing or commissioning of any roof covering, roof flashing, or any part of a roof drainage system. Plumbing registration/licensing is administered by the BPC. — [BPC – Roofing (stormwater) work](https://www.bpc.vic.gov.au/plumbers/plumber-classes-and-categories/roofing-stormwater-work); [VBA – Roofing (stormwater)](https://www.vba.vic.gov.au/plumbing/registration-licensing/roofing-stormwater)
- A roof drainage system usually comprises gutters, eaves and downpipes. — [VBA – Roofing (stormwater)](https://www.vba.vic.gov.au/plumbing/registration-licensing/roofing-stormwater)
- The national skill set "PLUMLICSS5 Plumbing Licence in Roofing (Stormwater) Work" is the training pathway. — [The Gordon – PLUMLICSS5](https://www.thegordon.edu.au/courses/all/plumlicss5-plumbing-licence-in-roofing-(stormwater)-work-skill-set)

**QLD (regulator: QBCC)**
- **Roof and wall cladding** licence covers selecting and installing roof cladding *other than terracotta and concrete tiles*, fabricating and installing rainwater goods, flashing penetrations (skylights, ventilators) and designing roof drainage components. — [QBCC – Roof and wall cladding](https://www.qbcc.qld.gov.au/licences/apply-licence/available-licences/other-trade/roof-wall-cladding)
- **Roof tiling** licence covers cutting and fixing roof tiles (concrete, clay, metal or similar), shingles and shakes, battens, bedding/pointing, flashing, safety mesh, sarking, anti-ponding boards and skylights. — [QBCC – Roof tiling](https://qbcc.qld.gov.au/licences/apply-licence/available-licences/other-trade/roof-tiling)
- Both licences include "incidental" work of other classes. Anything beyond incidental may need additional licences. Both come in contractor, company and site-supervisor forms. — [QBCC – Roof and wall cladding](https://www.qbcc.qld.gov.au/roof-wall-cladding); [ABLIS – QLD Roof and wall cladding licence](https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/qld/roof-and-wall-cladding-licence/42413)
- Advertising must include the licensee's (or company's) name and QBCC licence number. A trading name alone is likely non-compliant. Maximum penalty is 35 penalty units (about $6,044 for an individual, $30,222 for a company). — [QBCC – Advertising](https://www.qbcc.qld.gov.au/running-your-business/advertising)

**WA (regulator: Building and Energy, DMIRS/DEMIRS; Plumbers Licensing Board)**
- Building work over $20,000 that requires a building permit must be done by a registered building contractor. A roofer was fined $22,500 for $27,445 of unregistered roof and gutter work. — [WA Gov – Roofer fined $22,500 (Phillips Roof & Gutter Solutions)](https://www.wa.gov.au/government/announcements/slippery-slope-roofer-fined-22500-carl-phillips-and-phillips-roof-gutter-solutions-pty-ltd)
- Plumbing work requires a plumbing contractor's licence or tradesperson's licence from the Plumbers Licensing Board. — [Building and Energy – Plumbers licensing overview](https://commerce.wa.gov.au/building-and-energy/plumbers-licensing-overview); [ABLIS – WA Tradesperson's Licence (Drainage Plumbing)](https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/wa/tradespersons-licence-drainage-plumbing-/17153)

**SA (regulator: Consumer and Business Services, CBS)**
- A building work contractor's licence from CBS is required for any building work done for another person. Plumbing is building work but falls under separate plumbing, gas fitting and electrical (PGE) licences. — [CBS – Building work in SA: what you need to know](https://cbs.sa.gov.au/documents/Building-work-in-SA-what-you-need-to-know.pdf); [SA Gov – PGE contractors' licences](https://www.sa.gov.au/topics/business-and-trade/licensing/building-and-trade/licensing)
- Historic regulations made separate provision for PGE licences limited to "minor roof plumbing work". Current regulations exempt plumbing contractors from also needing a building work contractor licence (current wording not verified). New *Building Work Contractors Regulations 2026* appear on the legislation site. — [SA Building Work Contractors Regulations 2011 (current)](https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/_legislation-documents/lz/c/r/building-work-contractors-regulations-2011/current/2011.197.auth.pdf); [SA Building Work Contractors Regulations 2026](https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/_legislation-documents/lz/c/r/building-work-contractors-regulations-2026/current/2026.58.auth.pdf)
- Domestic building work contracts of $12,000 or more need a written contract. Building indemnity insurance is required where the price is $12,000 or more and approval is required. Unlicensed work penalties run up to $150,000 for an individual and $500,000 for a company. — [CBS – Building licence FAQs](https://cbs.sa.gov.au/documents/occlicensing/Building-licence-faqs.pdf); [HIA – Guide to licensing for building work in SA](https://hia.com.au/resources-and-advice/managing-your-business/managing-compliance/articles/guide-to-licensing-for-building-work-in-south-australia)

**TAS (regulator: Consumer, Building and Occupational Services, CBOS)**
- CBOS licenses a "Licensed Plumbing Practitioner – (Restricted) Restricted to Roof Plumbing work" category, which is within Automatic Mutual Recognition scope. — [Tas Treasury – Occupations within scope of AMR](https://www.treasury.tas.gov.au/Documents/Occupations_within_scope_of_AMR.pdf)
- A plumbing contractor licence is separately required to contract for plumbing work. — [CBOS – Plumbing contractor](https://www.cbos.tas.gov.au/topics/licensing-and-registration/licensed-occupations/plumbing/contractor); [ABLIS – Tas Plumbing Contractor Licence](https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/tas/plumbing-contractor-licence/7833)
- Plumbing work categories are set in the Occupational Licensing (Plumbing Work) Regulations 2021. — [Tas legislation – SR 2021-114](https://www.legislation.tas.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/sr-2021-114)

**ACT (regulator: Construction Occupations Registrar / Access Canberra)**
- Plumbing licences are issued under the Construction Occupations (Licensing) Act 2004. Companies and partnerships need a construction occupation licence. — [ABLIS – ACT Construction Occupation Licence – Company or Partnership](https://ablis.business.gov.au/ACT/pages/9035fd61-c6fc-4959-b8dd-f1c61ace9be5.aspx); [Trades Recognition Australia – Plumbers](https://tradesrecognitionaustralia.gov.au/plumbers)

**NT (regulator: Plumbers and Drainers Licensing Board)**
- The NT board states it "cannot issue licences in roof plumbing or mechanical services as these categories do not exist in the NT". — [NT Plumbers and Drainers Licensing Board – Registration](https://plumberslicensing.nt.gov.au/registration)

## Q2. Which plumbing or roof-drainage compliance certificates or notices of work must be lodged after roof plumbing jobs (thresholds and deadlines)?

### Takeaway
VIC requires a compliance certificate for plumbing work over $750, issued to the customer and lodged with the regulator within 5 days. NSW uses a Notice of Work at least 2 business days before work and a Certificate of Compliance within 2 business days after (7 if not inspected). Stormwater is often handled by council. QLD notifiable work goes on Form 4 within 10 business days, but QLD roof gutters are cladding-licence work, so Form 4 may not apply. Other states were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- **VIC**: Where total value of work (materials, appliances, labour, GST) exceeds $750, the licensed plumber must issue a compliance certificate to the customer within 5 days of completion (or of contract termination) and lodge it with the regulator within 5 days. — [VBA – Plumbing compliance certificates (consumer)](https://www.vba.vic.gov.au/consumers/home-renovation-essentials/plumbing-compliance-certificates); [VBA – Issuing compliance certificates](https://www.vba.vic.gov.au/plumbing/renewals-other-requirements/compliance-certificates)
- **VIC**: The BPC fines plumbers for not providing compliance certificates within 5 days. — [BPC news 2026 – drainage inspection requirements](https://www.bpc.vic.gov.au/news/2026/bpc-reminds-plumbers-of-drainage-inspection-requirements-after-plumber-hit-with-maximum-fine); [BPC – Infringement notices](https://www.bpc.vic.gov.au/about-bpc/compliance-and-enforcement/infringement-notices)
- **VIC**: Compliance certificates cannot be withheld for non-payment. — [VBA news 2019](https://www.vba.vic.gov.au/news/news/2019/compliance-certificates-cause-influx-of-complaints-but-cannot-be-withheld-for-non-payment)
- **NSW**: The Plumbing and Drainage Regulation 2017 requires a Certificate of Compliance within 2 business days after completing work, or 7 business days if the regulator has not inspected within the required window. — [NSW Plumbing and Drainage Regulation 2017 (in force 2024-11-05)](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/pdf/inforce/2024-11-05/sl-2017-0482)
- **NSW**: Notice of Work must be lodged at least 2 business days before work starts. Combined NoW/CoC forms are used for work not needing inspection. — [Gunnedah Shire – Plumbing & drainage](https://www.gunnedah.nsw.gov.au/index.php/development/building-construction/plumbing-drainage); [Eurobodalla – Plumbing approvals](https://www.esc.nsw.gov.au/property/building-And-certification/plumbing-approvals); [Upper Hunter – NoW/CoC form](https://www.upperhunter.nsw.gov.au/Plan-and-Build/Building-Planning-and-Development-Forms/Notice-of-Work-Certificate-of-Compliance-Plumbing-Drainage)
- **NSW stormwater**: some councils require Section 68 (Local Government Act 1993) approval for stormwater drainage before work. Some councils inspect stormwater only when they are the certifying authority. — [Lismore – Plumbing works](https://www.lismore.nsw.gov.au/Building-planning/Building-construction/Plumbing-works); [Hawkesbury – Plumbing & drainage inspections](https://www.hawkesbury.nsw.gov.au/plan-and-build/certification/plumbing-and-drainage-inspections)
- **QLD**: Notifiable work is plumbing/drainage work done without a council permit or mandatory inspection. It must be registered with the QBCC on Form 4 (Form 4A for public sector) within 10 business days of completion, with a fee (cited figures conflict: $35.67 / $36.88 / older $46.59). Plumbing associated with new construction is permit work, not notifiable. Categories are in Schedule 1 of the Plumbing and Drainage Regulation 2019. — [QBCC – What is notifiable work](https://www.qbcc.qld.gov.au/notifiable-work-plumbing-drainage/what-notifiable-work); [QBCC – Register notifiable work (Form 4/4A)](https://www.qbcc.qld.gov.au/notifiable-work-plumbing-drainage/register-notifiable-work-form-44a); [QBCC Form 4 PDF](https://Www.qbcc.qld.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/form-4-notifiable-work.pdf); [Scenic Rim – Form 4 fact sheet](https://scenicrim.qld.gov.au/downloads/file/4008/form-4-notifiable-works-fact-sheet)

### Inferences
- The SaaS should offer per-state post-job compliance tasks:
  - VIC: "Compliance certificate due (5 days) if value > $750", triggered on Roofing (stormwater) jobs.
  - NSW: "Notice of Work ≥2 business days before start" and "Certificate of Compliance ≤2 business days after (7 if uninspected)", plus "check council s68 stormwater approval".
  - QLD: "Form 4 within 10 business days" only where the job includes regulated plumbing/drainage (e.g. stormwater connection), not for rainwater goods alone.
- Compliance-certificate value thresholds should be configurable per state, because they change.

- Whether QLD roof gutters/downpipes ever count as notifiable work (Schedule 1 not retrieved).

## Q3. WHS for work at heights: SWMS triggers, state differences, edge protection and codes of practice

### Takeaway
Nationally, roofing with a risk of a fall of more than 2 m is high-risk construction work (HRCW) and needs a SWMS before work starts. South Australia, the last jurisdiction with a 3 m threshold, moved to 2 m on 1 July 2026. Victoria uses its own OHS Regulations 2017 (falls over 2 m, reg 44 hierarchy, reg 49 rescue procedure). Roof edge protection is referenced to AS/NZS 4994.2.

### Cited Findings
- Model WHS laws: construction work involving a risk of a person falling more than 2 m is HRCW, and a SWMS must be prepared before it starts. — [Safe Work Australia – HRCW requiring SWMS](https://safeworkaustralia.gov.au/duties-tool/construction/hazards-information/high-risk-construction-work-requiring-swms); [Safe Work Australia – Working at heights](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/hazards/working-heights)
- Model code of practice: "Managing the risk of falls in housing construction". — [Safe Work Australia – Model COP (docx)](https://safeworkaustralia.gov.au/system/files/documents/1810/model-cop-preventing-falls-in-housing-construction.docx); also "Safe work on roofs" info sheet — [Safe Work Australia – Safe work on roofs](https://safeworkaustralia.gov.au/system/files/documents/1702/safe-work-roofs-information-sheet.docx)
- **SA**: the HRCW fall threshold was lowered from 3 m to 2 m from 1 July 2026, aligning SA with the model laws. SafeWork SA reports two-thirds of falls occurred between 2 and 3 m. — [SafeWork SA – Lowering height threshold](https://safework.sa.gov.au/news-and-alerts/news/news/2025/lowering-height-threshold-will-raise-safety-standards); [CM3 – SA expands HRCW requirements](https://www.cm3.com.au/2026/07/sa-expands-work-at-height-requirements/). An older OHS Reps FAQ still says SA uses 3 m, which is now superseded — [OHS Reps – Working from heights](https://www.ohsrep.org.au/faqs/ohs-reps-@-work-other-/working-from-heights-what-are-the-regulations)
- **VIC**: Work with a risk of falling more than 2 m is HRCW, and work must not start until a SWMS is developed. OHS Regulations 2017 reg 44 sets the fall-control hierarchy (passive fall prevention first, then edge protection etc.). Reg 49 requires a documented emergency/rescue procedure. — [MBAV – Working from heights](https://mbav.com.au/working-heights); [OHS Reps – Prevention of falls](https://www.ohsrep.org.au/3_3_prevention_of_falls); [WorkSafe Vic – Worker critically injured after slipping off roof](https://www.worksafe.vic.gov.au/safety-alerts/worker-critically-injured-after-slipping-roof-and-falling-through-guardrails)
- Roof-edge temporary edge protection is specified by AS/NZS 4994.2. AS 4994.3:2023 covers edges other than roof edges. — [Vic Energy (VEU) – Edge protection guidance](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0042/784878/CS-2365_1.2_Edge-Protection_Working-at-Height_V1.pdf)
- **ACT**: WorkSafe ACT recommends top rail, mid rail and toe board where the potential fall exceeds 2 m. — [WorkSafe ACT – Stop construction falls: roofs](https://www.worksafe.act.gov.au/health-and-safety-portal/safety-topics/safety-advice/stop-construction-falls/roofs)
- **WA**: WorkSafe WA housing roof-fall guidance says a SWMS for 2–3 m work must explain why fall-prevention measures were not used, and it treats roof slopes over 26° as higher risk. It also notes WHS Regulations reg 79 will apply to HRCW from 1 October 2026 (exact change not verified). — [WorkSafe WA – Managing risk of falls in housing construction (2026)](https://www.worksafe.wa.gov.au/system/files/documents/2026-02/WS_BR_Managing-risk-falls-housing-construction-industry.pdf)
- Enforcement example: in WorkSafe Victoria v Transplumb Maintenance, a roofer was prosecuted for having no fall protection and no SWMS. — [Health & Safety Handbook – plumber falls 7 m](https://www.healthandsafetyhandbook.com.au/case-law/hefty-fine-after-plumber-falls-7-metres/)

### Inferences
- A single national rule works for the SaaS: "fall risk > 2 m → SWMS required before start" in all 8 jurisdictions from 1 July 2026, with VIC labelled under OHS Regs 2017 rather than WHS Regs.
- Job-check fields: roof height, pitch (flag > 26°), edge-protection type (AS/NZS 4994.2), brittle/fragile roof present, rescue/emergency plan (VIC reg 49 explicit), SWMS attached and signed.
- An older WA prosecution showing a 3 m threshold probably reflects the pre-2022 OSH Regulations 1996. WA has used the WHS Act 2020 and WHS (General Industry) Regulations 2022 since 31 March 2022 **[UNVERIFIED – background knowledge]**.

### Gaps
- Exact NSW, QLD, TAS and NT state-specific codes of practice for falls/roofs were not retrieved. All adopt the model WHS laws **[UNVERIFIED – background knowledge]**.
- The national white card (general construction induction) requirement was not sourced.
- The content of the WA "reg 79 from 1 Oct 2026" change is unclear.

## Q4. Asbestos: when is a licensed removalist required, and what notifications apply?

### Takeaway
Removing more than 10 m² of non-friable asbestos (e.g. AC roof sheeting), or any friable asbestos, needs a licensed removalist (Class B or A). The regulator must generally be notified at least 5 days before licensed removal. Victoria also requires notification at least 24 hours before smaller (≤10 m²) non-friable removal jobs.

### Cited Findings
- No licence is needed to remove less than 10 m² of non-friable asbestos. — [NSW Gov – Asbestos and demolition (AMR)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/licences-and-credentials/automatic-mutual-recognition/occupations/asbestos-and-demolition); [Comcare – Licensed asbestos removal work notification form](https://www.comcare.gov.au/about/forms-pubs/docs/forms/safety-and-prevention/licensed-asbestos-removal-work-notification-form.pdf)
- **WA**: licensed work is removal of any quantity of friable asbestos or more than 10 m² of non-friable. — [WorkSafe WA – Asbestos removal notifications](https://worksafe.wa.gov.au/asbestos-removal-notifications)
- **NSW**: notify SafeWork NSW 5 calendar days before licensed removal (more than 10 m² non-friable, or any friable). — [SafeWork NSW – Asbestos notifications](https://www.safework.nsw.gov.au/notify-safework/asbestos-notifications); [Service NSW – Notify SafeWork of asbestos removal](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/notify-safework-nsw-of-asbestos-removal-work)
- **SA**: licence holders must notify at least 5 days before licensed removal. — [ABLIS – SA Notification of asbestos removal](https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/sa/notification-of-asbestos-removal/46890)
- **VIC**: notify WorkSafe at least 24 hours before removal of 10 m² or less of non-friable ACM. Five days applies to licensed removal. — [WorkSafe Vic – Asbestos removal notification](https://www.worksafe.vic.gov.au/asbestos-removal-notification)
- **QLD, TAS, ACT, NT** notification pages: [WorkSafe QLD – Asbestos notifications](https://www.worksafe.qld.gov.au/safety-and-prevention/incidents-and-notifications/asbestos-notifications); [ABLIS – Tas notification of commencement](https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/tas/notification-of-the-commencement-of-asbestos-removal-work/46903); [ABLIS – ACT notice of asbestos removal work](https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/act/notice-of-asbestos-removal-work/46826); [NT WorkSafe – Asbestos notification](https://worksafe.nt.gov.au/notify-nt-worksafe/asbestos-notification); [ABLIS – QLD Class B licence](https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/qld/asbestos-removal-licence-b-class/4092)
- Even unlicensed (≤10 m²) removal must follow safe work procedures. — [NT WorkSafe – Asbestos licensing](https://worksafe.nt.gov.au/licensing-and-registration/asbestos)

### Inferences
- SaaS job check: "Built before 1990? → asbestos check / register sighted". Then: "AC roof area > 10 m² non-friable OR any friable → licensed removalist (Class B / A) + regulator notified ≥5 days before". For VIC also: "≤10 m² non-friable → WorkSafe notification ≥24 h before".
- Model WHS Regs: Class A = friable, Class B = >10 m² non-friable; 5-day notification under reg 466 **[UNVERIFIED – regulation numbers from background knowledge]**.

## Q5. Building permits and approvals: when does re-roofing need a permit, approval or consent?

### Takeaway
Like-for-like re-roofing with no structural change is generally exempt in NSW (Codes SEPP exempt development) and VIC (Building Regulations 2018 Schedule 3). Changing the roofing material (e.g. tile ↔ metal) or replacing structural battens generally needs a permit in VIC. Heritage items or overlays remove the exemptions. QLD councils issue exemption certificates for some re-roofing. Other states were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- **NSW**: The Codes SEPP lists roof tiling, sheeting and wall cladding repair or replacement as exempt development where it complies with the NCC and applicable standards (cl 2.54 referenced). Wall-cladding work is limited to 1–2 storey dwellings etc. and cannot use external combustible cladding. — [NSW Planning – Remedial building work SEPP fact sheet](https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-07/remedial-building-work-sepp-fact-sheet.pdf)
- **NSW**: the 2018 amendment replaced "re-cladding roofs or walls". Re-cladding must not involve structural alterations and must replace existing materials with similar materials (exceptions apply). — [NSW legislation – EPI 2018-505 (as made)](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/pdf/asmade/epi-2018-505); [Codes SEPP 2008 (EPI 2008-572)](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/pdf/asmade/epi-2008-572)
- **NSW**: earlier versions excluded heritage items or draft heritage items from the roof-cladding exemption. — [NSW legislation – EPI 2011-99](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/pdf/asmade/epi-2011-99)
- **VIC**: like-for-like replacement (e.g. tiles for tiles; corrugated iron for Colorbond/pre-finished sheet) does not need a building permit. Changing material (iron → concrete/terracotta tiles, or tile → iron) does. — [Hume City – Building permit exemptions](https://hume.vic.gov.au/Building-and-Planning/Building-and-Renovations/Building-Permit-Exemptions); [Monash – Roofing](https://www.monash.vic.gov.au/Planning-Development/Building/Renovations/Roofing); [Hepburn – Do I need a building permit](https://www.hepburn.vic.gov.au/Planning-building/Building-services/Building-permits/Do-I-need-a-building-permit)
- **VIC**: battens are structural members and are not exempt under Schedule 3 of the Building Regulations 2018. Re-sheeting is exempt only if no structural battens are replaced. Exempt work must still comply with the NCC and must not adversely affect structural soundness or safety. — [Inside Strata – Difficulties in roof replacement works](https://inside.strata.community/the-difficulties-in-roof-replacement-works/); [Hepburn – Do I need a building permit](https://www.hepburn.vic.gov.au/Planning-building/Building-services/Building-permits/Do-I-need-a-building-permit); [VBA/BPC – BP-01 When is a building permit required](https://www.building.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0031/583915/BP-01-When-is-a-building-permit-required.pdf)
- **QLD**: councils issue exemption certificates for re-roofing dwellings (example: Charters Towers EXE2023-0007). — [Charters Towers – Exemption certificate re-roofing](https://www.charterstowers.qld.gov.au/downloads/file/2571/exemption-certificate-exe2023-0007-re-roofing-dwelling-house-20-york-street-queenton-qld-4820)
- **WA**: roof and gutter work valued at $27,445 "required a building permit" in the Phillips prosecution, so WA re-roofing can be permit work. — [WA Gov – Roofer fined $22,500](https://www.wa.gov.au/government/announcements/slippery-slope-roofer-fined-22500-carl-phillips-and-phillips-roof-gutter-solutions-pty-ltd)

### Inferences
- Job check: "Like-for-like material? Structural battens/framing replaced? Heritage item/overlay? Bushfire-prone land? Cyclone region (QLD/WA/NT)?" Any "change/yes" answer → "Permit/approval likely required – confirm with certifier/council".
- QLD exemption-certificate practice suggests QLD re-roofing (especially in cyclonic regions where tie-downs must be upgraded) commonly needs certifier involvement.

## Q6. Technical standards a roofer must reference, and which regions they affect most

### Takeaway
The NCC (Vol. 2 Housing Provisions / Vol. 1) is the base requirement. It calls up AS 1562.1 (metal sheet roofing), AS 2050 (roof tiles), AS/NZS 3500.3 (stormwater, gutters, downpipes and box gutters), AS/NZS 4200 (pliable membranes/sarking) and AS 3959 (bushfire BAL). Cyclonic wind regions C and D (northern QLD, WA, NT) bring extra fixing and tie-down requirements, and some AS 1562.1 deemed-to-satisfy paths exclude them.

### Cited Findings
- NCC 2022 Vol. Two (H1P1) is satisfied for sheet roofing complying with AS 1562.1 (metal). — [NCC 2022 Vol. Two – H1 Structure (WA)](https://cpd.nccb.gov.au/editions/ncc-2022/adopted/volume-two/11-western-australia/h1-structure)
- ABCB: solutions for roofing and for gutters and downpipes are in AS 1562.1, AS 2050 and AS/NZS 3500.3. Box gutters were removed from the 2022 Housing Provisions so the complete solution sits in AS/NZS 3500.3. — [ABCB – NCC 2022 seminars: roof and wall cladding](https://abcb.gov.au/resources/videos/2022-ncc-seminars-volume-two-roof-and-wall-cladding)
- AS 2050:2018 is the installation standard for roof tiles. — [Standards (SAI) – AS 2050:2018](https://shop.standards.ie/en-ie/standards/as-2050-2018-98874_saig_as_as_207924/)
- Master Builders: inadequate tie-down/fixing is a common audit finding that leaves roofs exposed to cyclonic uplift. — [MBA – Ensure compliant roofing installations](https://mba.org.au/ensure-compliant-roofing-installations-to-avoid-expensive-rectification-works/)
- Product approvals often exclude cyclonic regions C and D (e.g. ventilation products "not tested for use in cyclonic wind regions C or D"). — [Windmaster BAL-40 product technical statement](https://pureventilation.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/windmaster-bal-40-product-technical-statement.pdf); [Lysaght – NCC compliance bulletin](https://cdn.dcs.lysaght.com/download/lysaght-permalite-ncc-compliance-bulletin-permalite)
- Bushfire: AS 3959:2018 requires roof ventilation openings etc. to be ember-guarded with non-combustible mesh at higher BALs (secondary source). — [BuildingTools – BAL-FZ metal roof rules](https://buildingtools.co/tool/fire-bushfire-roof-construction-bal-fz)
- VIC regulator research flags roofs and drainage as a major source of water ingress defects. — [VBA/BPC Fact Sheet PES-24 (Oct 2024) Water ingress](https://www.bpc.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0017/25019/VBA-Fact-Sheet-PES-24-October-2024-Water-ingress-in-buildings-Balconies,-roofs-and-drainage-Research-insights.pdf)
- Manufacturer installation manuals (e.g. Lysaght) are routinely required alongside the standards. — [Lysaght roofing & walling installation manual](https://cdn.dcs.lysaght.com/download/lysaght-roofing-walling-installation-manual)
- Cold-formed steel standards list (ASI). — [ASI – Standards and handbooks list](https://www.steel.org.au/Membership/media/Australian-Steel-Institute/PDFs/Standards-and-Handbooks-List-cold-formed-steel-construction-100325.pdf)

### Inferences
- Standards to reference in the SaaS, by job type:
  - Metal roof: NCC + AS 1562.1 + manufacturer manual + HB 39.
  - Tile roof: NCC + AS 2050 (+ AS 2049 tiles).
  - Gutters/downpipes/box gutters: AS/NZS 3500.3 (and NCC Housing Provisions for eaves gutters).
  - Sarking/underlay: AS/NZS 4200.1/.2.
  - Wind: AS/NZS 1170.2 / AS 4055 (housing).
  - Bushfire: AS 3959 BAL-LOW → BAL-FZ.
  - Edge protection: AS/NZS 4994.2.
- Region flags: cyclonic C/D → northern QLD, northern WA (Pilbara/Kimberley), NT Top End. BAL → bushfire-prone land in all states (heavily VIC, NSW, SA, WA, TAS, ACT).
