## 1. Main suppliers by category and state/territory coverage

### Takeaway
Metal roofing in Australia is dominated by BlueScope-owned networks (Lysaght, plus Metroll, which a low-quality source says BlueScope owns, and The Roofing Centre Tasmania), Stramit, Fielders, and the independent Steeline network. Tiles come mainly from CSR Monier (Saint-Gobain), Bristile Roofing (Brickworks) and the former Boral Roofing business (now owned by Lutum). Roof plumbing is dominated by Reece (676 ANZ branches) and Tradelink. A supplier list in the product should be **national brands plus state-scoped branches**, with a free-text "other supplier" option, because many roofers buy from local independents (for example No.1 Roofing in Sydney).

## 2. Pricing availability: public prices, trade price sheets, file formats, portals, APIs and integrations

### Takeaway
Trade pricing in Australia is **account-specific and sits behind a login**. Bunnings Trade states outright that the PowerPass price shown is the customer's own price, not a generic one. Plumbing merchants offer real downloads and integrations: Tradelink TradeDoor exports PDF, CSV, TAB or XML on a weekly or monthly schedule, and Reece maX connects directly to ServiceM8, AroFlo, Ascora, FieldPulse and Housecall Pro. Job-management tools (Tradify, ServiceM8, simPRO) import **supplier CSV files** with column mapping. Buildxact's supplier catalogues depend on the supplier loading prices and approving the connection. I found **no evidence of a public API or price feed from Lysaght, Stramit, Metroll or other roll-formers**. For metal roofing, the realistic path is an import of the roofer's own CSV, Excel or PDF quote.

### Inferences
- Supplier price files have **two tiers**:
  - **Plumbing/drainage merchants (Reece, Tradelink)**: structured downloads or APIs exist, but only through partner integrations or the user's own login.
  - **Roll-formers and roofing centres (Lysaght, Stramit, Metroll, Steeline, independents)**: no public feed was found. These suppliers most likely send price lists or quotes to account holders as PDF or Excel. **[UNVERIFIED; no source describes their trade price-list format]**
- MVP design: (1) CSV/XLSX upload with a column-mapping wizard and saved mappings per supplier, as Tradify and ServiceM8 do; (2) optional PDF-table extraction for roll-former PDFs; (3) an email-in import address per supplier, as ServiceM8 does; (4) later, a partnership integration with Reece maX and Tradelink TradeDoor, which need a formal partner agreement.
- Typical columns to support in the import mapping:
  - supplier product code
  - description
  - brand / range
  - profile (e.g. Custom Orb, Trimdek, Klip-Lok)
  - finish (Colorbond colour name / Zincalume / Colorbond Ultra / Matt)
  - BMT gauge (0.42 / 0.48 mm)
  - cover width (mm)
  - girth (mm) for flashings
  - unit of measure (LM, EA, BOX, ROLL, M2, LENGTH)
  - pack quantity
  - price ex GST
  - GST flag
  - effective date

  Tradelink's custom-format support for GST and GL codes ([NZ help](https://tradedoor.mico.co.nz/help/help_custom_price_formats)) suggests GST and tax-code columns are standard. Most of the column list is inferred, not documented by a roofing supplier.
- Price-change frequency: at least two merchant price-increase notices in 2026 (July and September), and Tradelink's weekly or monthly download scheduling. Price sheets should be stamped with "valid from" dates, and the product should warn when a sheet is older than about 60–90 days (my suggestion).

## 3. Legal and practical constraints on redistributing supplier price lists

### Takeaway
Trade prices are individual to each account. Bunnings Trade confirms this, and the other suppliers' terms are not public. Australian copyright protects the original expression and arrangement of a compilation, but not the underlying facts. Even so, redistributing one roofer's negotiated pricing to others would expose the product to confidentiality and contract claims, and to supplier hostility. **The product should treat each roofer's imported sheet as that tenant's private data, and should never pool or redistribute it.**

### Cited Findings
- Bunnings Trade: PowerPass prices are "the customer's own individual PowerPass price that is setup for their account", and no generic price is shown. — [Bunnings Trade price policy](https://trade.bunnings.com.au/policies/price-policy)
- In *IceTV v Nine Network* (HCA, 2009), the court focused on originality of expression. Commentators saw the decision as narrowing protection for factual compilations. — [Wikipedia: IceTV v Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IceTV_Pty_Ltd_v_Nine_Network_Australia_Pty_Ltd); [Arts Law](https://www.artslaw.com.au/article/the-latest-word-on-copyright-in-factual-compilations/)
- In *Telstra v Phone Directories* [2010] FCAFC 149, there was no copyright in the White and Yellow Pages: "sweat of the brow is no substitute for originality", and the compilation was produced largely by computer. — [Federal Court judgment](https://www.judgments.fedcourt.gov.au/judgments/Judgments/fca/full/2010/2010fcafc0149); [Brightlaw](https://brightlaw.com.au/?p=14426)
- Copyright can subsist in a compilation but not in the facts it contains. — [Mondaq: Can a database be protected by copyright?](https://www.mondaq.com/australia/intellectual-property/290668/can-a-database-be-protected-by-copyright)
- FieldPulse's Reece CMP feature notes that customer-facing prices will not show the trade rate. Integrators therefore protect trade rates from end customers. — [FieldPulse CMP](https://help.fieldpulse.com/integrations-partners/suppliers-distributors/reece-max-integration-anz/reece-max-integration-cmp-pricing-anz)
- The Reece integrations (AroFlo, Ascora, Housecall Pro) all use the user's own Reece credentials. Pricing is delivered per account, not shared. — [Ascora](https://ascora.com.au/home/reecemax); [Housecall Pro](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/10844040-how-to-use-the-reece-integration-by-housecall-pro)

### Inferences
- Copying the facts (individual prices) may not breach copyright after IceTV. However, copying a supplier's formatted PDF or catalogue (descriptions, images, layout) could. Trade account terms almost certainly include confidentiality and acceptable-use clauses **[UNVERIFIED; no supplier T&Cs were retrieved]**.
- Product rules:
  - per-tenant price books
  - no cross-tenant pricing analytics or "market price" aggregation without explicit opt-in and anonymisation
  - customer-facing quotes show sell price, not cost
  - an import attestation ("I am authorised to use this price list")
  - store a source file reference and effective date
  - bundled default catalogues should hold product specs only (profiles, cover widths, BMT), never prices
- Any direct supplier feed (Reece maX, TradeDoor) needs a formal partner agreement. The existing integrations are all vendor partnerships.

### Gaps
- No supplier trade terms (Lysaght, Stramit, Reece, Tradelink) were retrieved to confirm the confidentiality wording.
- No Australian case specifically on price lists was found. This needs legal advice.

## 4. Common product units and mapping to an estimating calculator

### Takeaway
Metal roof sheeting is sold per lineal metre, cut to length, by profile × finish × BMT. The calculator converts roof area (m², adjusted for pitch) into sheet count × sheet length using the profile's **cover width**. Ridges, hips, barges, valleys, flashings and gutters are sold per lineal metre, or per stock length, so the calculator's lineal-metre measurements map straight onto them, plus laps and waste. Sarking is sold per roll (e.g. 1350 mm × 30 m or 60 m), tiles per tile or by tiles-per-m², and screws per box.

### Cited Findings
- **Custom Orb (Lysaght)**:
  - cover width 762 mm; rib height 17 mm
  - mass 3.32 kg/m at 0.42 mm BMT and 3.76 kg/m at 0.48 mm BMT (Colorbond)
  - sold at 0.42 and 0.48 mm thicknesses, with online stores showing "price per lineal metre"

  — [Lysaght Custom Orb](https://lysaght.com/profiles/custom-orb); [Custom Orb brochure](https://cdn.dcs.lysaght.com/download/custom-orb-and-custom-blue-orb-brochure); [Roof shop Custom Orb Colorbond](https://rsc-roofshopv1.azurewebsites.net/custom-orb-colorbond)
- **Sarking sold per roll with dimensions**:
  - Bradford Thermoseal Roof Metal Wall 1350 mm × 30 m and 1350 mm × 60 m
  - Bradford Enviroseal HTS 1500 mm × 30 m
  - Sisal Enviro Heavy Duty 1350 mm × 60 m

  — [Canterbury Timbers 30 m](https://www.canterburytimbers.com.au/products/sisal-roof-metal-light-duty-roof-wall-30m); [60 m](https://www.canterburytimbers.com.au/products/sisal-roof-metal-light-duty-roof-wall-60m); [Enviroseal](https://www.canterburytimbers.com.au/products/bradford-enviroseal-proctorwrap-htr-1500mm-x-50m); [Sisal Enviro HD](https://www.canterburytimbers.com.au/products/sisal-enviro-heavy-duty-1350mm-x-60m)
- **Anticon blankets** are sold in rolls of several thicknesses, with roll size and area listed. — [Mitre 10 Bradford guide PDF](https://www.mitre10.com.au/media/wysiwyg/BrandPages/Bradford/TheBlock2024/BrandHub2024BradfordProductGuideBrochureDownload.pdf)
- **Ridge capping and gutters** are quoted per lineal metre in cost guides. — [QuoteCheck 2026](https://quotecheck-blog.pages.dev/posts/roofing-cost-australia-2026); [TheQuoteYard NSW gutters](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/gutter-replacement-nsw)
- **Tiles**: cost guides quote per m² (material and installed), and tile repairs per tile. — [Bricks & Agent roof tiling](https://www.bricksandagent.com/blog/roof-tiling-cost); [QuoteCheck](https://quotecheck-blog.pages.dev/posts/roofing-cost-australia-2026)

### Inferences (calculator mapping; my formulas, not from a source)
- **Pitch factor** = 1 / cos(pitch°). True roof area = plan area × pitch factor. Rafter (sheet) length = horizontal run × pitch factor + overhang into the gutter (~50 mm, **[UNVERIFIED typical]**).
- **Metal sheets** (UOM = LM):
  - sheets per roof plane = ceil(plane width along eaves / cover width), e.g. 0.762 m for Custom Orb
  - LM = sheets × sheet length
  - waste: round up for hips and cut sheets; cut sheets on hip planes can be estimated as area ÷ cover width + 5–10% **[assumption]**
  - the price key is profile + finish (Colorbond colour vs Zincalume vs Ultra) + BMT
- **Ridge, hip and barge capping** (UOM = LM or per stock length): LM = measured length × (1 + lap allowance). If the sheet prices per length (e.g. 3.0 m or 6.0 m pieces), pieces = ceil(LM / (length − lap)).
- **Flashings** (UOM = LM, priced by girth in mm and number of bends): the product needs a "girth" attribute on each flashing line, and a valley flashing is matched by girth.
- **Valleys**: LM measured, plus valley clips where needed.
- **Gutters and downpipes** (UOM = per length, e.g. 5.8 m or 6.5 m **[UNVERIFIED typical stock lengths]**, or LM): eaves LM → gutter lengths, plus stop ends, outlets and brackets each.
- **Screws** (UOM = BOX of 100/500/1000): count = sheets × purlin crossings × screws per crossing. Pack count must be a column in the import.
- **Sarking** (UOM = ROLL; coverage m² = width × length, e.g. 1.35 × 60 = 81 m²): rolls = ceil(true roof area × (1 + overlap ~150 mm ÷ roll width) / roll m²). Overlap per manufacturer instructions **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Tiles** (UOM = EA or M2): tiles = true area × tiles-per-m² (a profile-specific attribute from the manufacturer's data sheet), and ridge tiles = ridge LM × ridges per LM.
- **Hire** (skip, scaffold, edge protection): UOM = EA per job, or per week, so use flat line items with a duration input.
- The importer must normalise UOM synonyms: "LM / L/M / per m / lin m", "EA / each", "BX / box", "RL / roll", "LGTH / length".

### Gaps
- Exact column layouts of Lysaght, Stramit or Metroll trade price sheets. No sample was found.
- Standard stock lengths for capping and gutters by supplier, tiles per m² by profile, and screw-pack sizes for Buildex roofing screws. None were found in the search budget.
