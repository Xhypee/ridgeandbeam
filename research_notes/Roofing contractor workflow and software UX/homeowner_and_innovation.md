# Homeowner Experience of Hiring a Roofer and Emerging Product/UX Innovation in Roofing and Field-Service Software

Method note: this is based on about 20 web searches. Direct page fetches of roofingcontractor.com and roofr.com failed: the session's network settings do not allow those hosts. Findings from those domains therefore come from search-result snippets, not full-page reads. Most quantitative claims below come from vendors (software or SMS companies) and are flagged as such.

## Q1. Homeowner pain: trust and scams, price transparency, quote comparison, communication, scheduling, mess, insurance confusion, payment/financing, and what homeowners review roofers on

### Takeaway
Homeowners rank price clarity first, then trust and professionalism (license, insurance, warranties, an inspection before the quote), then communication during the job. The biggest documented harms come from storm-chaser and insurance-claim tactics, and from deposits taken for work never done. Several states now write consumer protections into law: deductible-waiver bans and 72-hour cancellation rights. A roofing SaaS can build these into its contract and proposal flows.

### Cited Findings
**What homeowners want (industry surveys)**
- Roofing Contractor's 2025 Homeowner Survey (sponsored by Roofle and Owens Corning): 65% of homeowners are more likely to call a roofer who shows pricing on its website; 66% say they would gather three quotes; referrals remain the main way homeowners find a roofer; reputation, licensing, warranties and material quality drive the decision. Snippet-level data only; full report not fetched. — [Roofing Contractor 2025 survey](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/100649-2025-homeowner-roofing-survey-tracking-the-journey); [IRE 2025 session preview](https://roofingcontractor.com/articles/100470-homeowners-survey-2025-a-customers-journey)
- Roofing Contractor also ran 2024 and 2026 editions of the survey, plus an article titled "What homeowners actually want from their roofer, and it's probably not what you think." These are useful for trend lines but were not readable in this session. — [2024 survey](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/99486-2024-homeowner-roofing-survey); [May 2026 edition](https://digitaledition.roofingcontractor.com/may-2026/homeowner-survey/); [article](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/102503-what-homeowners-actually-want-from-their-roofer-and-its-probably-not-what-you-think)
- Roofr survey of 292 US homeowners (vendor; branded as a 2026 homeowner report): pricing ranked as the top factor; professional proposals and estimates were a trust signal; 74% would not hire a roofer without an inspection first; homeowners say they would pay more for a roofer who keeps them informed. — [Roofr: What do homeowners want from roofers](https://roofr.com/masterclass/what-do-homeowners-want-from-roofers); [Roofr Homeowner Report 2026](https://roofr.com/gated-content/homeowner-report-2026)

**Cost and financing context**
- Angi reports average US homeowner project spending of $12,472 in 2025, with maintenance and emergency-repair spending up. — [Angi 2025 State of Home Spending highlights](https://intercom.help/angi/en/articles/13464134-angi-s-2025-state-of-home-spending-report-highlights)
- In Angi's 2024 report, 11% of respondents had a new roof installed and 10% had roof repairs. Inflation pushed homeowners toward financing; younger homeowners lean toward credit cards and refinancing, older ones toward savings. — [Angi 2024 State of Home Spending](https://angi.com/press/2024-state-of-home-spending-report)
- Fixr puts 2025 replacement costs at $15,160–$27,580, typically about $21,050, and says 40% of homeowners prefer to pay for roofing by credit card. That second figure is not independently verified. Other sources cite about $10,000 "per Angi," so the cost benchmarks conflict. — [Fixr roofing statistics](https://www.fixr.com/articles/us-roofing-industry-statistics); [Finder roof financing](https://finder.com/personal-loans/roof-financing)
- Contractor financing may carry a lien on the home until the loan is repaid, a disclosure homeowners may not expect. — [Finder roof financing](https://finder.com/personal-loans/roof-financing)

**Scams, storm chasers and consumer-protection guidance**
- FTC guidance (as relayed by Scripps "Don't Waste Your Money" segments): walk away from contractors who demand cash upfront, are vague about where they are based, or won't provide copies of their license and insurance. — [Scripps / 10News](https://www.10news.com/money/consumer/dont-waste-your-money/spring-storm-season-is-here-how-to-avoid-a-weather-related-scam)
- BBB, after the April 2026 storms: a reputable roofer is likely to be busy, which gives homeowners time to call their insurance agent, document damage and do research. Not all storm chasers are scammers, but they may lack licensing or overpromise. — [KY3, Apr 29 2026](https://www.ky3.com/2026/04/29/your-side-better-business-bureau-warns-roofing-scammers-after-this-weeks-storms/); [KBTX, Mar 2025](https://www.kbtx.com/2025/03/16/severe-weather-brings-storms-scammers/)
- Recommended reporting channels: ReportFraud.ftc.gov and state contractor licensing boards. Legitimate roofers rarely knock doors right after a storm, and homeowners should get legal advice before signing an assignment of insurance benefits. — [RooferDirectory storm-chaser guide 2026](https://rooferdirectory.co/articles/storm-chaser-roofing-scams/); [Goodville Mutual](https://www.goodville.com/blog/734495601/Storm%20Chasers%20&%20Roofing%20Scams:%20How%20to%20choose%20a%20reputable%20contractor)

**State laws that shape contract and proposal UX**
- Deductible waivers are banned in several states. Kansas bars advertising or promising to pay or rebate any part of a deductible. Texas makes it illegal, with up to a $2,000 fine and 6 months in jail, and the Texas AG runs a hotline. In Minnesota, an insurer need not consider the estimate of a contractor who violates the rule. — [Kansas HB 2307](https://kslegislature.gov/li_2012/b2011_12/measures/documents/hb2307_00_0000.pdf); [Texas deductible law, Roofing Contractor](https://roofingcontractor.com/articles/93855-texas-legislature-takes-a-stand-against-roofing-contractors-that-waive-deductibles); [Minnesota session law](https://revisor.mn.gov/laws/2010/0/Session+Law/Chapter/324)
- There are 72-hour rescission rights. South Dakota allows cancellation within 72 hours of signing. In Colorado and Minnesota, the homeowner may rescind within 72 hours of the insurer's written notice that the claim is denied in whole or in part. — [South Dakota 2012 ch. 198](https://mylrc.sdlegislature.gov/api/Documents/SessionLaw/22820.html?Year=2012); [Minnesota session law](https://revisor.mn.gov/laws/2010/0/Session+Law/Chapter/324); [JLC: Roofers face a flood of new state laws](https://www.jlconline.com/how-to/roofing/roofers-face-a-flood-of-new-state-laws_o/)

**What homeowners complain about (BBB, Reddit and forums)**
- BBB received more than 6,000 complaints about roofers in 2019, and roofers ranked in the top 10 complaint categories in parts of Missouri and Illinois. Recurring complaints are deposits taken with little or no work done and contractors who are then unreachable, poor workmanship (one roof left unattached blew off), and insurance billing disputes, such as being billed the deductible on top of the insurance payout. — [News Tribune / BBB tips (2020)](https://www.newstribune.com/news/business/story/2020/apr/05/bbb-tips-think-safety-first-when-hiring-roofers/823221/)
- Individual BBB profiles show complaints about debris and cleanup, such as shingles thrown into the attic space, and deductible billing. BBB notes that how complaints are handled matters more than how many there are. — e.g. [BBB roofing complaint profile (Clovis, NM)](https://www.bbb.org/us/nm/clovis/profile/roofing-contractors/m-quality-construction-llc-0806-99135081/complaints)
- On Reddit (snapshots via a mirror), homeowners describe door-knockers declaring older roofs "totaled" and offering contracts that are void if insurance doesn't pay for a full replacement. Others are suspicious of roofers eager to "deal with insurance, not me." Commenters report adjusters cutting bids, payouts weeks late, depreciation and deductible cutting payments roughly in half, and premiums rising after a claim (one reported +$500/yr). Anecdotal, unverified. — [Reddit snapshot 1](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1tk31y5/snapshots/2026-05-23T02%3A07%3A01.891785Z); [Reddit snapshot 2](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1shtno6/snapshots/2026-04-11T01%3A45%3A56.841673Z); [BiggerPockets: roofer started work before insurance approval](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/44/topics/460571-roofer-started-work-before-told-to-by-insurance); [Policygenius: lessons from the home insurance subreddit](https://www.policygenius.com/homeowners-insurance/lessons-from-the-home-insurance-subreddit/)

### Inferences
- Trust is the product. A homeowner-facing layer that shows license and insurance certificates, the crew and company identity, a local address, reviews, and an "inspection first" record directly answers the top fears (storm chasers, no-show after deposit).
- Insurance claims are the most confusing part of the journey, with deductible, depreciation/ACV vs RCV, supplements, payment timing, and who talks to the adjuster all in play. A plain-language claim timeline with "what happens next / who owes what" is a clear gap. The pain shows up in Reddit and BBB disputes; no vendor in this research showed a homeowner-facing claim explainer.
- Compliance can be a selling point. State-aware contracts with the required 72-hour cancellation notice, automatic blocking of deductible-waiver language, and deposit-limit guardrails protect both the roofer and the homeowner.
- Communication during the job and cleanup (nails, debris) recur in complaints, which supports proactive status texts and a post-job "magnet sweep / cleanup complete" photo checklist.

### Gaps
- No current, national BBB breakdown of roofing complaint categories was found; the best data is from 2019–2020 or individual profiles.
- Could not read full survey reports (Roofing Contractor 2024/2025/2026, Roofr 2026) because the hosts are blocked in this session, so sample sizes, methodology and secondary stats such as financing use and preferred communication channel are unconfirmed.
- No systematic study of Google or Angi review text for roofers (for example, the frequency of "communication," "clean," "nails" mentions) was found. Direct Reddit r/Roofing and r/HomeImprovement threads could not be fetched; only mirror snapshots were seen.
- The FTC's own storm-chaser page was not surfaced; FTC guidance here is relayed via news segments.

## Q2. Customer-facing experiences in the market: instant quotes, portals, live job tracking, photo reports, digital proposals with options, financing in the proposal, review requests. What converts?

### Takeaway
Instant website quotes (Roofle, Roofr Instant Estimator), multi-option good/better/best proposals with embedded financing, and fast proposal delivery are the standard conversion plays. Most lift numbers are vendor claims or anecdotes. Solar installers offer the most mature model for homeowner-facing milestone tracking, and roofing has not matched it.

### Cited Findings
**Instant quotes**
- Roofle Roof Quote PRO, sold through Beacon/QXO, claims an 8–10% average conversion of form completions, with some customers above 15% (vendor). — [Beacon: Roofle](https://becn.com/roofle); [QXO: Roofle](https://www.qxo.com/roofle)
- A Roofle case study claims a 12% average conversion vs a 2% baseline for typical home-services landing pages (vendor/agency). — [Coherent Solutions case study](https://www.coherentsolutions.com/case-studies/roofle)
- Roofle testimonial pages claim close rates "as high as 80%" and a "7,104% ROI" (anecdotal marketing). — [Roofle testimonial](https://offers.roofle.com/rqp-matthew-mcdaniel)
- Roofr Instant Estimator is a paid add-on (listed at about $125/month) that lets homeowners get instant quotes and captures leads from a website, business cards or marketing materials. Roofr began as a YC S17 marketplace with instant satellite estimates. — [GetApp: Roofr](https://www.getapp.com/roofing-software/a/roofr); [YC blog](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/roofr-is-a-roofing-marketplace-with-instant-satellite-roofing-estimates-yc-s17)
- This aligns with the 2025 survey finding that 65% of homeowners are more likely to call a roofer with pricing on its website. — [Roofing Contractor 2025 survey](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/100649-2025-homeowner-roofing-survey-tracking-the-journey)

**Proposals, options and financing**
- NRCIA describes good/better/best as the professional proposal standard, with higher tiers tied to longer workmanship or manufacturer warranties. — [NRCIA](https://www.nrcia.org/tag/homeowner/page/2/)
- Roofr markets multi-option proposals as a core feature. — [Roofr multi-option proposals](https://roofr.com/masterclass/multi-option-proposals); [Roofr proposals](https://roofr.com/proposals)
- In a financing anecdote, a roofer offering financing on every quote saw close rate on $10K+ jobs rise from 27% to 38% and average job size grow from about $11.4K to $15.8K over 90 days (single vendor case study). — [ContractorPlus](https://contractorplus.app/blog/how-a-roofer-closed-41-more-jobs-over-10k-by-offering-financing-on-every)
- On proposal speed, vendor blogs claim proposals sent within 24 hours close at 2–3x the rate of those sent 3–5 days later; primary data was not verifiable. Residential close-rate benchmarks are cited at roughly 25–30%, with top performers at 40–50%+. — [US Tech Automations](https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/automate-stop-proposals-taking-too-long-in-roofing-2026); [SubcontractorHub](https://www.subcontractorhub.com/blog/roofing-sales-close-rate)

**Job tracking, portals and photo reports**
- Freedom Forever (solar) runs a customer-portal timeline where completed milestones are solid green and future ones are outlined, with a self-help guide explaining each step. — [Freedom Forever portal timeline guide](https://intercom.help/freedomforever/en/articles/12045186-how-to-read-the-customer-portal-timeline-self-help-guide-for-project-updates); [PDF](https://freedomforever.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Timeline-Help-Guide-Customers.pdf)
- In Reonic (solar CRM), the installer chooses which internal statuses the customer sees, and each status moves forward automatically as the project advances. — [Reonic docs](https://docs.reonic.com/docs/en/settings-customer-communication-progress-tracking)
- Bodhi's solar app connects to the installer's CRM and texts and emails the homeowner at every step from design to power-on. Typical milestones are permit submitted and approved, equipment delivered, install start and completion, inspection, and utility interconnection. — [Electrek on Bodhi](https://electrek.co/2022/01/31/this-app-makes-tracking-rooftop-solar-installation-and-performance-a-cinch-for-homeowners/); [Fieldproxy solar milestone automation](https://www.fieldproxy.ai/automations/solar-installation-milestone)
- Housecall Pro's automated texts cover job scheduled, "On my way," job started and job finished. Customer replies go to the assigned tech's app, and the wording can be customized on higher plans. — [Housecall Pro customer notifications](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/357613-customer-notifications-overview); [HCP: what notifications customers receive](https://help.housecallpro.com/notifications/what-job-notifications-will-my-customers-receive)
- CompanyCam–Hover integration: Hover photos sync to the CompanyCam project and the Hover report lands in Files; the homeowner can be invited to add photos. — [CompanyCam help](https://help.companycam.com/en/articles/6828439-integrate-companycam-hover)

**Channel preference**
- Tinuiti home-services study: 58% prefer to schedule home services by phone, about 3x the next option (online). 37% of Gen Z and millennials prefer online or email vs 17% of boomers. Text is framed as the follow-up channel. — [Tinuiti home services study](https://reports.tinuiti.com/tinuiti-home-services-marketing-study/page-4.html)
- EZ Texting 2024 (an SMS vendor): SMS is consumers' top channel for utility messages such as appointment reminders, and text (35%) edges email (31%) and phone (29%) for customer support. — [EZ Texting 2024 report](https://www.eztexting.com/report/2024-consumer-texting-report); [press release](https://www.eztexting.com/press/press-releases/2024-consumer-behavior-texting-report-ez-texting-shows-text-now-dominant)

### Inferences
- What likely converts is the combination: instant ballpark price on the website, inspection, a same-day multi-option proposal with monthly payment shown per tier, then e-sign. Each step answers a stated homeowner preference (price first, inspection first, professional proposal).
- Roofing lacks a "Domino's tracker" equivalent. Solar portals show the pattern to copy: installer-curated milestones, auto-advance from field check-offs, and SMS at each step. For roofing, the milestones would be inspection, insurance or adjuster, contract, material ordered, permit, material delivered, tear-off, dry-in, install complete, cleanup and magnet sweep, final photos, invoice, warranty registered.
- Hybrid channels: offer phone booking with a fast human or AI answer, then text-first for confirmations, "on my way," day-of updates and photos.

### Gaps
- No independent, controlled data on the conversion lift from instant quotes, multi-option proposals or embedded financing; all figures are vendor-sourced.
- No data found on review-request timing or volume effects specific to roofing.
- Could not verify Tesla or Sunrun app specifics.

## Q3. Emerging tech 2024–2026: AI measurement, AI damage detection, AI receptionists and speed-to-lead, AI proposals and supplements, drones, voice, offline-first, text-first

### Takeaway
AI receptionists became table stakes in 2025: Jobber, Housecall Pro and Roofr all ship one, at roughly $99/month in roofing tools. Satellite or photo measurement is mature (Roofr, Hover, EagleView). AI damage detection (EagleView Assess) and AI supplements and estimates (RoofGenius) are emerging, but accuracy is disputed. Vendors are converging on "AI operating systems" that span intake to payment (Zuper for Roofing, Hover's January 2026 unified platform).

### Cited Findings
- Jobber launched Receptionist (AI, answers calls and texts) on August 25, 2025. It is included in Plus plans and an add-on for Grow. A later update extended it to assist existing clients. — [DestinationCRM](https://www.destinationcrm.com/Articles/CRM-News/CRM-Across-the-Wire/Jobber-Launches-Receptionist-171139.aspx); [Jobber AI Receptionist](https://www.getjobber.com/ai-receptionist/); [Jobber product updates](https://productupdates.getjobber.com/121638-receptionist-now-does-more-to-assist-your-existing-clients)
- Housecall Pro "CSR AI" answers forwarded calls and web chat. A third-party review lists texting as "coming soon" (unverified). — [UseCarly review](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/housecall-pro-ai-receptionist/)
- Roofr AI Receptionist is reported at $99/month for 200 minutes. JobNimbus has "AssistAI"; AccuLynx's AI adoption is described inconsistently, from "no AI features" to "AI lead scoring" (competitor-authored comparison blogs). — [Alivo: best roofing CRMs 2026](https://www.alivo.ai/blogs/the-best-roofing-crms-in-2026); [Zuper: best AI roofing CRM](https://www.zuper.co/blog/best-ai-roofing-crm-software)
- Zuper launched "Zuper for Roofing" in September 2025, billed as an AI operating system unifying lead intake, inspections, proposals, signing, scheduling, production tracking, invoicing and payments. — [BusinessWire, Sept 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250928585122/en); [syndicated release](https://am590theanswer.com/news/business/zuper-launches-zuper-for-roofing-the-ai-operating-system-transforming-one-of-nor/4e5a932d9d1247e6b783858eb38dc767)
- EagleView Assess expanded drone roof inspection with AI-driven damage-detection models; the vendor claims fewer false positives and recalls. — [RoofersCoffeeShop](https://www.rooferscoffeeshop.com/post/eagleview-assess-expands-drone-roof-inspection-solution-to-include-large-and-complex-roofs-ai-driven-models-underpin-offering)
- Hover launched Instant Design (AI photorealistic designs from one photo) in February 2025, interior and virtual walkthrough tools in April 2025, and in January 2026 a unified visual platform covering scan, scope, design, estimate and proposal. — [BusinessWire Apr 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250402052052/en); [BusinessWire Jan 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260113669531/en); [Hover news](https://hover.to/news)
- RoofGenius AI bundles AI supplements (claim recovery), an AI estimator (branded proposals) and instant satellite measurements. — [GetApp: RoofGenius AI](https://www.getapp.com.au/software/2085302/roofgenius-ai)
- CompanyCam Premium reportedly auto-generates job summaries and reports from field photos (secondary source). — [ToolEdUpPro review](https://tooleduppro.pages.dev/reviews/companycam)
- A Colorado Roofing Association piece and a ServiceTitan blog both caution that AI damage detection misses subtle issues and produces false positives. — [Colorado Roofing Association](https://www.coloradoroofing.org/news/ai-in-roofing-practical-applications-colorado-contractors-should-know-about); [ServiceTitan: AI in roofing](https://www.servicetitan.com/blog/roofing-ai)
- On speed to lead, ServiceTitan reports only about 50% of contractors respond to new leads within an hour. Hatch (vendor) says 88% of HVAC contractors take more than 5 minutes. The widely repeated "21x more likely to connect within 5 minutes" figure traces to older InsideSales/Harris-era research that could not be confirmed. A vendor study of 2,847 contractor leads claims 47% booking within 60 seconds vs 4% after 30+ minutes (unverified). — [ServiceTitan: speed-to-lead AI](https://www.servicetitan.com/blog/speed-to-lead-ai-virtual-agent); [CustomerFlows study](https://customerflows-website.pages.dev/research/contractor-lead-response-time-study/); [Contractor Mag](https://www.contractormag.com/best-practices/5-steps-close-lead-management-gap)

### Inferences
- A new entrant cannot win on "has an AI receptionist" alone; that is now parity. It could stand out with AI that hands off cleanly into the roofing workflow: the AI books the inspection, pre-pulls a satellite measurement, and drafts a multi-option proposal before the rep arrives.
- AI damage detection and supplements carry trust risk with homeowners and insurers given false positives. Present AI findings as rep-reviewed evidence (photo plus annotation), not as automatic conclusions.
- AI photo reports that turn CompanyCam-style photos into a homeowner-readable summary are a low-risk, high-value homeowner touchpoint.

### Gaps
- No independent accuracy benchmarks for AI damage detection or AI measurement versus manual measurement were found.
- Voice-input-in-field features (dictated notes or measurements) at roofing vendors were not specifically documented in the results.
- Dated 2026 AI announcements from AccuLynx, JobNimbus and Roofr were not found.

## Q4. UX patterns from best-in-class field-service apps worth borrowing (one-tap actions, glove-friendly, outdoor readability, automations, offline)

### Takeaway
The patterns that matter are one-tap status actions that trigger customer messages (Housecall Pro's "OMW"), automated lifecycle texts, large glove-friendly touch targets, high-contrast screens readable in sunlight, and offline-first sync with explicit sync states. Field-tool sources agree on these, though most guidance is vendor-authored.

### Cited Findings
- One tap on Housecall Pro's "On my way" texts the customer. Admins can uncheck "Notify customer" per job, and customer replies route to the assigned tech's app. — [Housecall Pro customer notifications](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/357613-customer-notifications-overview); [HCP notification settings](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/14171435-customer-notifications-in-settings)
- Jobber sends automatic updates, follow-ups and on-my-way texts as standard job notifications. — [Jobber AI receptionist page](https://www.getjobber.com/ai-receptionist/)
- Standard touch sizes fail with gloves, so navigation and error handling need redesign, not just bigger buttons. Interfaces need high contrast because "a screen that looks fine indoors is unreadable in direct sunlight." — [Apario: software for people who work outside](https://blog.apario.net/on-writing-software-for-people-who-work-outside); [App Fabric: field workforce UX](https://app-fabric.pages.dev/blog/2024-03-field-workforce-ux); [Glance: designing apps for construction workers](https://thisisglance.com/learning-centre/how-should-i-design-apps-for-construction-workers)
- Offline-first means the app fully works without signal and syncs in the background, with explicit states such as "Offline – will sync later" and "Conflict detected." The suggested acceptance test is to open a work order, switch on airplane mode, then take a photo, add a note and capture a signature. — [GeekyAnts CX chapter](https://customer-experience.geekyants.com/book/it_services/chapter_21.md); [Team400: field service mobile apps 2025](https://team400.ai/blog/2025-07-field-service-mobile-apps)
- Other recommendations: thumb-reach navigation, minimal typing, smart defaults, and surfacing context (customer notes, parts, the start-job button) when a tech marks arrival. Battery drain is a deal-breaker. Technicians work around software that doesn't fit their workflow. — [Fieldproxy: mobile-first technician app](https://www.fieldproxy.ai/blog/mobile-first-technician-app-why-your-field-teams-will-actually-use-it-d1-32); [MangoApps: why technicians work around FSM software](https://www.mangoapps.com/articles/why-technicians-work-around-field-service-software)
- Solar pattern: field crews check off phases in the mobile app, which updates the customer portal, sends SMS and flags delays. — [Fieldproxy solar blueprint](https://www.fieldproxy.ai/blueprints/solar-installation-progress); [Reonic docs](https://docs.reonic.com/docs/en/settings-customer-communication-progress-tracking)

### Inferences
- Design principle: every field action is also a customer message. One tap on arrived, tear-off started, dry-in, done or cleanup verified should update the homeowner tracker and send a templated text with photos, and the rep should be able to suppress it.
- Roof-specific constraints go beyond generic field service: steep pitch, so one-handed use; gloves, heat and glare; poor signal in new subdivisions; and photo-heavy workflows. Offline photo capture with queued upload and visible sync state should be core, not optional.
- Pair the AI receptionist with "speed-to-lead" automations: instant text acknowledgment, then a booking link, then a satellite measurement pre-pulled for the rep.

### Gaps
- Primary research with authoritative numbers (minimum glove touch-target size, sunlight contrast ratios) was not found; vendor guidance should be checked against Apple and Material guidelines and field testing.
- No direct evidence on ServiceTitan's or CompanyCam's specific mobile UX patterns (e.g., CompanyCam's capture flow) beyond feature descriptions; their help docs were not fetched.
- Tesla Solar/Energy app specifics were not found.
