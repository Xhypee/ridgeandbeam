# Roofing Contractor Software: User Sentiment and Role-Specific Pain Points

> **Method and reliability notes (read first).** This session's network blocked direct page fetches. Everything below comes from web-search results, which return short excerpts and quotes from the pages and not the full text. ## Q1. What users praise and complain about, by product (UX focus)

### Takeaway
Roofing-specific CRMs (AccuLynx, JobNimbus) are generally liked (about 4.2-4.6 stars). The complaints that come up most are: (1) price, add-ons and contract or renewal terms, especially Leap and ServiceTitan; (2) slow or crashing mobile apps, especially when uploading photos (AccuLynx); (3) learning curve and complexity; and (4) inaccurate measurements or poor coverage (Roofr, aerial reports in general). Support quality is the main thing that separates vendors: AccuLynx is praised for it, while Roofr and the forum posters describe weak support.

### Cited Findings

**AccuLynx**
- Capterra has roughly 800-850 reviews, ease of use averages about 4.4/5, and the search summary showed 95% overall positive sentiment. Capterra's theme summary says support is the standout: "93% positive" out of 148 reviews mentioning it. — [Capterra AccuLynx reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/)
- **Pattern: price.** Capterra's theme summary shows pricing at about 71% negative across 66 reviews that mention it, and a reviewer called add-on costs expensive. — [Capterra AccuLynx reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/)
- **Pattern: mobile app instability and slow photo uploads.**
  - Capterra (review excerpt): the app "often crashes for all our employees" and "it can be hard to navigate jobs and reports." — [Capterra AccuLynx reviews p.4](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/?page=4)
  - Capterra (review excerpt): "downloading and taking pictures takes forever on the app. Also the app freezes a lot." — [Capterra AccuLynx reviews p.9](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/?page=9)
  - App Store (4-star review excerpts): the app is well built and responsive for measurements and notes, but it "seems to not allow me to upload photos and this changes often with updates." A second 4-star review says it "sometimes freezes or crashes, especially when uploading multiple photos." — [AccuLynx Field, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/acculynx-field/id1485611392)
  - An affiliate source (2026) calls the app "exceedingly slow, buggy, and has a poor overall layout" even after the Fall 2025 mobile-estimating update, and says Android users report more performance problems than iOS users. — [roofingsoftwareguide AccuLynx review](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/reviews/acculynx-review/) (affiliate)
- **Recurring: customization limits and manual re-entry.** One reviewer wished AccuLynx "was more customizable" and had to move bookings from third-party sites into it by hand. Other cons: "cumbersome" payments and invoicing, the system being "too complex," and dislike of a messaging redesign. — [Capterra AccuLynx reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/)
- **Anecdote:** AccuLynx does not notify users when an email arrives on a job. — Capterra review, surfaced via [Capterra compare page](https://www.capterra.com/compare/116187-126797/Acculynx-vs-JobNimbus)
- **Integration praise:** a roofing estimator says photos taken in CompanyCam sort automatically into the customer's profile in AccuLynx. — [Software Advice CompanyCam profile](https://www.softwareadvice.com/field-service/companycam-profile/)

**JobNimbus**
- Capterra: 4.6/5 from about 480 verified reviews. G2: 4.6 from 119 reviews, compared with AccuLynx at 4.2 from 36. — [Capterra JobNimbus](https://capterra.com/p/126797/JobNimbus/reviews/?page=2); [G2 comparison](https://www.g2.com/compare/jobnimbus-vs-quick-bid-vs-acculynx)
- **Recurring cons:** a steep learning curve that can require extra training, a buggy calendar, and occasional sync or performance problems. Quote-only pricing is reported as the top pricing complaint. — [Capterra JobNimbus reviews](https://capterra.com/p/126797/JobNimbus/reviews/?page=4); [tomba.io pricing review](https://tomba.io/blog/jobnimbus-pricing-reviews-pros-and-cons) (secondary)
- Comparison blogs describe JobNimbus as easier to learn and faster to set up than AccuLynx. They say AccuLynx has deeper roofing integrations, such as material ordering, plus job costing, P&L and commission tracking. — [Projul](https://projul.com/blog/best-acculynx-alternatives) (competitor); [JobNimbus vs AccuLynx](https://www.jobnimbus.com/comparison/jobnimbus-vs-acculynx) (vendor; it concedes AccuLynx has job costing, P&L, commissions and a production calendar that JobNimbus lacks)

**Roofr**
- **Praise (pattern):** low cost, fast turnaround, and reports that are "the easiest to read of all of the reports" (review excerpt). One user said Roofr's measurements were "very close" to those from other aerial providers. — [Software Advice Roofr reviews](https://www.softwareadvice.com/construction/roofr-profile/reviews/); [Capterra Roofr](https://www.capterra.com/p/208102/Roofr/)
- **Complaints (recurring):**
  - A 2-star review says measurements and roof pitches are "regularly incorrect." The same reviewer reports a price increase and a subscription he could not cancel.
  - Other reviewers say support is "severely lacking" and the site is "cumbersome" to use.
  - Rural addresses, tree cover and stale imagery produce flat 2D reports with no pitch.
  - Flashing is miscalculated, for example step and wall flashing applied to chimneys.
  - Sources: [Software Advice Roofr reviews](https://www.softwareadvice.com/construction/roofr-profile/reviews/); [fervorstudio Roofr review](https://fervorstudio.ca/news/roofr-review-pricing-alternatives/) (secondary); [toricentlabs](https://toricentlabs.com/blog/roofr-alternative.html) (competitor)
- **Mobile:** sources say there is no native App Store app, the progressive web app needs a connection with no true offline mode, and users report glitches with night mode, creating measurements on mobile, and navigation. — [fervorstudio](https://fervorstudio.ca/news/roofr-review-pricing-alternatives/); [rooferbase](https://www.rooferbase.com/blog/roofr-software-what-roofers-need-to-know-in-2025) (competitor). *Not independently verified. Check the App Store and Google Play directly.*
- **Pricing (secondary):** Pro about $99/month and Premium $169/month, plus $13-19 per measurement report. — [fervorstudio](https://fervorstudio.ca/news/roofr-review-pricing-alternatives/)
- **Switching (anecdote):** one Capterra reviewer moved from Roofr to AccuLynx because they saw AccuLynx as "a more complete system." — via [Capterra AccuLynx p.2](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/?page=2)

**Leap (SalesPro)**
- **Pattern in negative reviews: price hikes and contract lock-in.**
  - Capterra (review excerpt): "The price hike became 3x what I used to pay and they forced me to pay it for two months despite my never agreeing to this price adjustment." The bill reportedly went from about $55/month to a $99 base fee plus per-user charges.
  - Another reviewer reports paying four more months after cancelling in writing, citing "contract obligation."
  - Sources: [Capterra Leap reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/162690/Leap/reviews/)
- **Contract terms:** a one-year term with 60-day written notice to avoid auto-renewal. Leap can move fees to then-current list price with notice; sources disagree on whether that notice is 60 or 30 days. Pricing is quote-only; SalesPro Premium reportedly starts at $750/month for 6 users. — [fervorstudio Leap review](https://fervorstudio.ca/news/leap-salespro-review-pricing-alternatives/); [roofingsoftwareguide Leap pricing](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/guides/leap-pricing/) (affiliate). *Sources conflict on the notice period.*
- **Praise:** quoting and e-signature in the home, and fast support response that keeps "downtime" low. Some users mention bugs, such as uploads disappearing. — [Capterra Leap](https://www.capterra.com/p/162690/Leap/)

**ServiceTitan (for roofers)**
- **Pattern: contract lock-in and painful implementation.**
  - G2 (review excerpt): "it did not meet our needs, and we are no longer using the software while remaining obligated under the contract." — [G2 ServiceTitan reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/servicetitan/reviews)
  - Secondary sources report a 12-month minimum, no free trial, auto-renewal traps (one contractor missed a 30-day window and was "locked in for another 24 months"), early-termination fees of $5k-$20k+ (one case was $46k), and implementation fees of $5k-$50k+ with onboarding of 3-12 months.
  - A BBB complaint (Dec 2024) says the customer was never onboarded but paid for a full year.
  - Sources: [runacall contract review](https://www.runacall.com/learn/articles/servicetitan-contract-review-what-youre-actually-signing); [skillmammoth](https://skillmammoth.com/blog/servicetitan-reviews); [myquoteiq](https://myquoteiq.com/best-servicetitan-alternative-for-roofing-contractors/) (competitor)
- **Fit:** described as built for HVAC, plumbing and electrical, and a poor fit for roofers under about 15 technicians or $2M in revenue. — [roofingsoftwareguide ServiceTitan](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/reviews/servicetitan-roofing-review/) (affiliate)

**Jobber**
- **Praised** for ease of use and for scheduling, quoting and invoicing in one place. **Criticized** for limited reporting, paid add-ons for marketing tools, and few roofing-specific features. — [Quo blog](https://www.quo.com/blog/roofing-business-software/); [Zuper](https://www.zuper.co/blog/best-roofing-crm-2025) (competitor)
- A vendor page claims that r/roofing and r/smallbusiness threads "consistently mention Jobber" as the default for crews not ready to commit to a roofing-only tool. — via [followupcrm](https://www.followupcrm.com/blog/jobber-vs-acculynx-vs-jobnimbus) (vendor claim, not verified against Reddit)

**CompanyCam**
- High ratings: about 4.7 on review sites, 4.8 on iOS and 4.6 on Android. **Pattern:** cost rises steeply as the team grows. Seat math as of 2026: Core $79/month or $63 billed annually for 1 user, Crew $149 for 3 users, Scale $299, extra seats about $29-34. Other complaints: occasional performance problems, it is "just a photo tool," and uploads stall in rural areas with poor signal. — [fieldcamp](https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/companycam/) (competitor); [roofingsoftwareguide CompanyCam](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/reviews/companycam-review/) (affiliate); [theroofingbrief](https://theroofingbrief.com/companycam-review/)

**SumoQuote, RoofSnap**
- **SumoQuote:** the interface is easy for people new to CRMs, but reviewers "consistently highlight" mobile limitations and the product lacks multi-phase or milestone tracking. — [Zuper](https://www.zuper.co/blog/best-roofing-crm-2025) (competitor)
- **RoofSnap:** sources conflict. An affiliate guide reports a "steep learning curve, according to past users," while RoofSnap's own blog claims it is easy to learn. It is mostly a measurement and estimating tool with limited job management, and Capterra has only about 11 reviews. — [roofingsoftwareguide RoofSnap](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/reviews/roofsnap-review/); [RoofSnap blog](https://roofsnap.com/blog/roofsnap-reviews-roi-is-it-worth-it-for-your-roofing-business/) (vendor); [Capterra RoofSnap](https://www.capterra.com/p/174071/RoofSnap/reviews/)

**EagleView and Hover (measurement)**
- **EagleView** is seen as the standard insurers accept ("highest adjuster acceptance"). Its 98.77% accuracy claim comes from a benchmark it commissioned, with an undisclosed sample size. It publishes no price sheet; estimates range from $15-38 to about $87 for Premium at low volume. Turnaround is 24-48 hours, or about 3 hours at a rush premium, though sources conflict on this.
- **Hover** builds a 3D model from phone photos taken on site. It handles tree cover better, turns around in hours, and is useful in the sales conversation. Pricing: Starter is $29 plus pay-per-scan, and Pro is reportedly $999/year.
- **Pattern:** tree canopy is the main failure point for aerial reports.
- Sources: [theroofingbrief EagleView vs Hover vs RoofSnap](https://theroofingbrief.com/eagleview-vs-hover-vs-roofsnap/); [roofingsoftwareguide EagleView vs Hover](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/comparisons/eagleview-vs-hover/); [estimateondemand accuracy comparison](https://estimateondemand.com/roof-measurement-reports-accuracy-roofers/). *All are secondary or affiliate.*

### Inferences
- Contract and pricing trust is a UX issue for this market. The angriest reviews (Leap, ServiceTitan, Roofr) are about unexpected price increases and being unable to cancel, not about features. Transparent month-to-month pricing and self-serve cancellation could set a new product apart.
- Uploading photos on a weak signal is the most-cited mobile failure across AccuLynx, CompanyCam and Roofr. A field app needs offline-first capture, background upload queues with visible progress and retry, and should never lose a photo.
- Measurement accuracy, and adjusters accepting the report, drive trust in estimates. Users want a fallback when aerial imagery fails: on-site capture, manual pitch override, and visible confidence indicators.

### Gaps
- Full review text, review dates and reviewer roles for Capterra, Software Advice and TrustRadius could not be fetched because those domains were blocked in this session.
- No verbatim App Store or Google Play reviews for JobNimbus, Roofr, Leap, CompanyCam or Hover were retrieved.
- No YouTube reviews by roofing owners were surfaced.

## Q2. Why roofers switch or abandon software, and why some stay on spreadsheets, whiteboards or paper

### Takeaway
Roofers who switch most often cite cost and contract or renewal terms, poor support and training, the product not fitting roofing (general field-service tools), or needing a "more complete system." Forum posters say they have "tried a few but have not really been happy." There is little direct first-hand evidence on why roofers stay on spreadsheets and whiteboards; the reasons below are mostly inferred.

### Cited Findings
- RoofersCoffeeShop forum posts on CRM choice (as excerpted in search; dates are relative to indexing):
  - "We have tried a few but have not really been happy."
  - "Sometimes simple is best."
  - "Most of them do very little support and training."
  - Source: [RCS forum: What is your roofing company using for their CRM?](https://www.rooferscoffeeshop.com/forum/topic/what-is-roofing-company-using-for-their-crm)
- A second RCS thread: the original poster wanted QuickBooks integration and mobile drawing. A reply asked whether a one-stop solution exists or whether you must stitch several tools together. — [RCS forum: BEST CRM Software](https://www.rooferscoffeeshop.com/forum/topic/best-crm-software)
- Examples of switching:
  - A Capterra reviewer moved from Roofr to AccuLynx for "a more complete system."
  - Another reviewer used AccuLynx, then went to Leap "for a couple months."
  - Source: [Capterra AccuLynx p.2](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/?page=2)
- Leaving because of a contract or price hike: see the Leap and ServiceTitan findings above ([Capterra Leap](https://www.capterra.com/p/162690/Leap/reviews/); [G2 ServiceTitan](https://www.g2.com/products/servicetitan/reviews)).
- Fit: general field-service tools (ServiceTitan, Jobber) lack roofing workflows such as measurements, supplements and material orders. — [roofingsoftwareguide](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/reviews/servicetitan-roofing-review/) (affiliate); [Quo](https://www.quo.com/blog/roofing-business-software/)
- The maintenance burden of a whiteboard is described in general terms, with no roofing-specific evidence: someone has to "continually update the whiteboard." — [Wikipedia: SharePoint Dashboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SharePoint_Dashboard) (weak, generic source)

### Inferences
- Roofers end up with a patchwork of tools: a CRM, plus CompanyCam, plus EagleView or Hover, plus QuickBooks. The "is there a one-stop solution?" question shows the frustration of re-entering data across them.
- "Sometimes simple is best" and complaints about the learning curve suggest small shops drop complex CRMs when nobody owns setup or data hygiene. Good default workflows and minimal required configuration matter.

### Gaps
- No first-hand Reddit or Facebook-group posts on staying with spreadsheets, whiteboards or paper were found. Reddit could not be fetched, and site-restricted searches returned nothing. This needs direct Reddit access or user interviews.
- There are no quantitative churn data (for example, survey percentages of reasons for switching).

## Q3. Role-specific pains

### Takeaway
Most first-hand evidence covers sales reps (commission transparency, payment lag on insurance jobs, speed of estimates and proposals) and crews (photo uploads, language). Evidence specific to owners and office admins came mostly from vendor content and is thin.

### Cited Findings

**Owner**
- Owners pay attention to price, add-on creep, per-user cost growth (CompanyCam seat math; AccuLynx add-ons), contract risk, and wanting job costing and P&L visibility. JobNimbus's own comparison concedes AccuLynx has job costing, P&L and commission tracking, which signals these are expected features. — [Capterra AccuLynx](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/); [JobNimbus vs AccuLynx](https://www.jobnimbus.com/comparison/jobnimbus-vs-acculynx)
- An owner on a forum wanted QuickBooks integration, meaning accounting sync to avoid double entry. — [RCS forum](https://www.rooferscoffeeshop.com/forum/topic/best-crm-software)

**Office admin / production manager**
- **Anecdotes:**
  - Re-entering bookings from third-party sites by hand. — [Capterra AccuLynx](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/)
  - No notification when an email arrives on a job, so the office has to check. — via [Capterra compare](https://www.capterra.com/compare/116187-126797/Acculynx-vs-JobNimbus)
  - "Cumbersome" invoicing and payments. — [Capterra AccuLynx](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/)
  - A buggy calendar (JobNimbus). — [Capterra JobNimbus](https://capterra.com/p/126797/JobNimbus/reviews/?page=4)

**Sales rep**
- Reps often complain about receiving commission checks "without a detailed breakdown" or about delayed payment. One rep says the final insurance payment can take about 2-3 months. — [Contractors Cloud commission guide](https://contractorscloud.com/blog/roofing-sales-commissions-models-examples-payouts-and-how-to-automate-them/) (vendor blog; anecdote)
- Supplements complicate commissions: insurance money or supplements may still be outstanding at install. A common model pays part at install and trues up the rest after the supplement is paid. Reps want visibility into the job-cost lines behind profit-based commissions. — [Contractors Cloud](https://contractorscloud.com/blog/roofing-sales-commissions-models-examples-payouts-and-how-to-automate-them/); [Sequifi](https://sequifi.com/blog/roofing-sales-commission-structure) (vendor)
- A rep-facing advice site: if they can't give you a written commission agreement, "RUN." — [roofingsalesman.com](https://www.roofingsalesman.com/commission-percentage/)
- Speed in the field: reps value Hover's same-visit 3D capture and Leap's in-home quote plus e-signature. They complain about turnaround and accuracy of measurement reports and about mobile limits in SumoQuote and Roofr. — see the Q1 sources

**Crew lead / installer**
- Photo upload is slow, crashes or freezes, especially with many photos or a weak signal. — [AccuLynx App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/acculynx-field/id1485611392); [Capterra AccuLynx p.9](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/?page=9); [roofingsoftwareguide CompanyCam](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/reviews/companycam-review/)
- Language: vendors are adding Spanish work orders, which shows demand.
  - Roofr translates the full work order, including line items and quantities, into Spanish above English. — [Roofr product blog](https://roofr.com/product-blog/new-in-roofr-spanish-work-orders)
  - The AccuLynx Crew App translates in both directions between English and Spanish; crews can view the labor order with instructions and photos, and accept or reject the job. — [AccuLynx Crew App](https://acculynx.com/mobile-crew-app/); [AccuLynx language barriers](https://acculynx.com/how-roofing-contractors-can-eliminate-language-barriers-with-crews/)
  - *Vendor sources only; no first-hand crew quotes.*

### Inferences
- The best opportunity for reps is a commission view, live per job, that shows contract value, costs, supplements pending or paid, collected amount, and the payout schedule.
- For crews: a minimal, bilingual work order showing materials, quantities, special instructions and photos, an offline photo queue, and a single "material missing or problem" button that alerts the office.

### Gaps
- No first-hand owner quotes on cash flow, margins or hiring were found.
- No first-hand office-admin quotes on chasing paperwork or insurance follow-ups were found.
- No first-hand crew quotes on instructions or missing materials were found. These need Reddit, Facebook groups or interviews.

## Q4. Specific frustrations: mobile on the roof, field-to-office handoff, supplier ordering, supplements, weather scheduling, getting paid

### Takeaway
The best-documented frustrations are mobile photo capture and upload in poor conditions, and getting paid on insurance jobs (supplement lag that affects commissions). There was little or no evidence on scheduling around weather or on supplier-ordering pain.

### Cited Findings
- **Mobile on the roof:**
  - Crashes and freezes, especially during multi-photo upload. — [App Store AccuLynx Field](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/acculynx-field/id1485611392)
  - Roofr's progressive web app reportedly has no offline mode. — [fervorstudio](https://fervorstudio.ca/news/roofr-review-pricing-alternatives/)
  - CompanyCam uploads stall in rural areas. — [roofingsoftwareguide](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/reviews/companycam-review/)
  - Android performs worse than iOS (AccuLynx). — [roofingsoftwareguide AccuLynx](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/reviews/acculynx-review/)
- **Field-to-office handoff:** praise for CompanyCam photos syncing automatically into AccuLynx job profiles shows the value of zero-effort handoff. — [Software Advice CompanyCam](https://www.softwareadvice.com/field-service/companycam-profile/)
- **Supplier ordering:** AccuLynx is described as having a more established material-ordering workflow than JobNimbus. — [Projul](https://projul.com/blog/best-acculynx-alternatives) (competitor). No first-hand complaints about ordering were found.
- **Insurance supplements and getting paid:**
  - Final insurance payments take about 2-3 months, and supplements remain outstanding at install. — [Contractors Cloud](https://contractorscloud.com/blog/roofing-sales-commissions-models-examples-payouts-and-how-to-automate-them/)
  - EagleView reports are preferred because adjusters accept them. — [theroofingbrief](https://theroofingbrief.com/eagleview-vs-hover-vs-roofsnap/)
  - AccuLynx payments and invoicing are called "cumbersome." — [Capterra AccuLynx](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/)
- **Weather scheduling:** no sources were found.

### Inferences
- Treat the supplement-to-payment timeline as a primary object in the product, with status tracked from claim through supplement, approval and payment, and visible to owner, office and rep.
- Mobile work on the roof calls for large tap targets, single-handed capture and offline queuing. This is inferred from the upload complaints; no glove or sunlight-specific evidence was found.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found on scheduling crews around weather, supplier ordering and delivery errors, or sunlight readability and glove use on the roof. These need direct Reddit or Facebook research or field interviews.
- Network blocks prevented reading full reviews. Allowing capterra.com, softwareadvice.com, trustradius.com, rooferscoffeeshop.com and reddit.com would allow a deeper and verified pass.
