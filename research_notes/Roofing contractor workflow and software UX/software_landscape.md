# Roofing Software Landscape: Products, Features, Pricing, Positioning and Integrations

Research date: 2026-11-06. Method note: vendor sites (roofr.com, jobnimbus.com) could not be fetched in this session because of network restrictions, so most figures come from search-result summaries of third-party review and pricing sites and from news outlets. Many of those sites (roofingsoftwareguide.com, contractortoolstack.com, projul.com, myquoteiq.com, builderlync.com, ketterly.com, toricentlabs.com) are run by or tied to competing vendors. Treat their pricing as estimates unless it is marked vendor-published. Every pricing figure below should be checked against the vendor's own page before it is used in positioning material.

## For each product: target customer, core features, pricing model, contract terms and integrations

### Takeaway
Pricing splits into three groups. (1) Roofr, Jobber, Leap CRM and Contractors Cloud publish pricing or have well-reported prices. (2) The roofing incumbents (AccuLynx, JobNimbus, ServiceTitan, Buildertrend) are quote-only, with annual contracts and in some cases large implementation fees. (3) Measurement vendors charge per report: roughly $9-13 for RoofSnap, $13-19 for Roofr, $18-20 for GAF QuickMeasure, about $18-87 for EagleView and $25-139 per Hover project. Roofr's flat company price with unlimited users is the main pricing contrast to the per-seat incumbents. A transparent flat or low-seat price is still a real opening, because the dominant CRMs hide their pricing.

### Inferences
- **Background feature matrix (from general industry knowledge, not verified in this session):**
  - AccuLynx, JobNimbus and Leap each cover CRM, pipeline, proposals, e-sign, production scheduling, photos, invoicing and payments, reporting, a mobile app and supplier ordering integrations (ABC Supply, SRS, Beacon).
  - AccuLynx and JobNimbus also offer financing partners and commission tracking.
  - Roofr bundles measurements with a CRM and proposals.
  - CompanyCam, Hover, EagleView, GAF QuickMeasure and RoofSnap are point tools that integrate into the CRMs.
  - Xactimate is required for insurance-restoration work.
  - The report writer should mark this matrix "verify", because no vendor feature pages could be fetched.
- **Per-seat pricing is the norm among incumbents.** AccuLynx at about $100-250 per user, ServiceTitan per technician, Leap at $99 per additional user and Contractors Cloud per active user all bill by seat. Roofr's unlimited-user flat pricing is the clearest alternative and suits crews with many field users.
- **Measurement reports are a recurring per-job cost** of roughly $9-$87. A built-in roof calculator that cuts paid report use (for example a sketch or pitch tool on satellite imagery) is an obvious value lever for Ridge & Beam. Integrations with Hover, EagleView and Roofr imports will still be expected.
- **Onboarding fees and annual contracts are common.** Contractors Cloud charges a $950 activation fee, ServiceTitan $5k-$50k, and AccuLynx and Leap use annual contracts. "No contract, no setup fee, live same day" is a credible differentiator for small roofers.

### Inferences
- **The Verisk deal carries risk for an insurer-aligned AccuLynx.** If it closes, Xactimate's owner would control the leading roofing CRM. Rival CRMs and contractors may fear preferential Xactimate integration, and the FTC theory and the "ended talks with a competitor about enhanced integration" finding point the same way. ## How the products differ in philosophy (all-in-one vs best-of-breed, sales-focused vs production-focused)

### Takeaway
The market splits into roofing-only all-in-ones (AccuLynx, JobNimbus), sales and proposal-first tools (Leap SalesPro, SumoQuote, Roofle, SalesRabbit), measurement-first tools moving up into CRM (Roofr), point tools (CompanyCam, Hover, EagleView, GAF QuickMeasure, RoofSnap), generalist field-service platforms (Jobber for small crews, ServiceTitan for large firms) and generalist construction PM (Buildertrend). Insurance work pulls everything toward Xactimate compatibility.

### Cited Findings
- AccuLynx is described as "the roofing-only platform built to earn its price" — [contractortoolstack AccuLynx](https://contractortoolstack.com/software/acculynx/)
- Leap SalesPro is positioned as contractor estimating and proposal software for in-home sales — [Leap SalesPro](https://leaptodigital.com/salespro/)
- SalesRabbit is pursuing a unified "Find / Quote / Build" platform: canvassing, then Roofle online quotes, then RoofLink production — [SalesRabbit insights](https://salesrabbit.com/insights/salesrabbit-acquires-roofle/); [contractortoolstack AI estimating](https://contractortoolstack.com/categories/ai-estimating/)
- Roofr is measurement-led. Reports and ESX export are central to its value, with CRM, proposals and Sites layered on top — [contractortoolstack Roofr](https://contractortoolstack.com/software/roofr/)
- Hover vs. Roofr is framed as "photos or satellites": smartphone 3D capture vs. aerial imagery — [contractortoolstack Hover vs Roofr](https://contractortoolstack.com/compare/hover-vs-roofr/)
- ServiceTitan suits large or multi-trade operations better than small roofers and has long implementations — [roofingsoftwareguide ServiceTitan alternatives](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/roundups/servicetitan-alternatives-roofing-contractors/)
- Buildertrend is built around selections and remodeler PM and gets a roofing-specific review — [roofingsoftwareguide Buildertrend for roofers](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/reviews/buildertrend-review-roofing-contractors/)
- CompanyCam is criticised for "bloat" as prices rise for what is mainly a photo tool — [scanmanifold](https://www.scanmanifold.com/blog-posts/companycam-bloat-2026-what-you-are-paying-for); [fieldcamp "Just for photos?"](https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/companycam/)

### Inferences
- Typical stacks:
  - Mid-size insurance roofer: AccuLynx or JobNimbus, plus EagleView or Hover, plus Xactimate, CompanyCam and QuickBooks.
  - Small retail roofer: Roofr or Jobber, plus QuickBooks.
- Ridge & Beam (CRM, estimating with roof calculator, jobs, invoicing) competes most directly with Roofr (measure-to-proposal) and the lower tiers of JobNimbus. Its gap opportunity is to combine roof math, estimate, production and invoice in one flat-priced product without per-report fees.

## Which products are dominant for small (1-5 person) vs mid-size vs large roofers

### Takeaway
- Small (1-5 people): Roofr (free or low-cost, unlimited users), Jobber, Leap CRM Essential ($79) and pay-per-report measurement tools (RoofSnap, GAF QuickMeasure).
- Mid-size: JobNimbus and AccuLynx dominate, with Leap SalesPro for in-home sales teams.
- Large and multi-trade: AccuLynx at the top of its tiers, ServiceTitan, and enterprise measurement contracts (EagleView volume deals, Hover Business/Enterprise).

This sizing is mostly inferred from price points and review-site guidance, not from market-share data.

### Cited Findings
- A guide aimed at small contractors ($2-6M revenue) compares options for that segment — [toricentlabs small contractors](https://toricentlabs.com/blog/best-roofing-software-small-contractors.html)
- ServiceTitan's cost of $1,225-$1,990/mo for 5 technicians plus implementation is described as fitting large operators — [roofingsoftwareguide ServiceTitan pricing](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/guides/servicetitan-pricing-roofing/)
- AccuLynx at $100-$250 per user means $1,000-$2,500/mo for 10 users before add-ons — [Ketterly](https://ketterly.com/competitor-pricing/acculynx)
- Roofr's $0 Starter and unlimited users make it accessible to solo roofers — [roofingsoftwareguide Roofr pricing](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/reviews/roofr-pricing/)
- Jobber's Core plan ($29-$49, 1 user) targets solo operators — [WorkZen](https://www.workzen.io/en/blog/jobber-pricing-explained/)
- Hover Business ($2,999/yr, up to 10 branches, SSO) and EagleView 12-month volume commitments target multi-branch firms — [contractortoolstack Hover](https://contractortoolstack.com/software/hover/); [theroofingbrief](https://theroofingbrief.com/2026-aerial-roof-measurement-software-report/)

### Inferences
- **The 1-5 person segment is price-sensitive and underserved by roofing-specific all-in-ones.** AccuLynx and JobNimbus are quote-only with annual contracts, and Jobber lacks roof measurement and insurance workflows. Roofr is the main roofing-specific competitor at this size. Ridge & Beam should benchmark against Roofr Essentials ($249) and Jobber Connect ($99-$139).
